# Monster Recon: footage shot list

What to record in the Explorer for the footage posts, and how.

## Tips

- Required: one clean in-world 16:9 screenshot, 1920x1080 or larger, with no UI and no text, just gameplay with people playing.
- Recommended: 2-4 more 16:9 gameplay stills, at least 1920x1080, for social posts, the newsletter and press.
- Optional trailer: under 30 s, 16:9 1920x1080, with people in motion in the first three seconds.
- Trailer vertical cut: 9:16 1080x1920, under 30 s, for mobile and social.
- Trailer captions or on-screen text so it works with the sound off.
- Record in the Decentraland desktop Explorer at 1920x1080 or higher; keep the cursor out of frame.
- The Memory Match board is screen UI: record it at full screen so the cards and the timer read on a phone.
- For the chase, use third person and keep the hopping monster in the middle third of a 16:9 frame so a 9:16 crop keeps it.
- Play with sound on: the match, countdown and Halloween stingers carry the clips.
- Use Select checkpoint to replay an early level for the chase (12 s per hop) or a late one for panic (3 s per hop).
- Keep the two Epic monsters out of public clips: stop before the last checkpoints and close the Codex before the Epic row is visible.

## Shots

### Footage: MATCH!  (`footage-01`)

Start a board from checkpoint 1 at full screen. Flip pairs quickly so two or three "MATCH!" popups float up in a row, then clear the board to "Board complete!". First-person screen capture, sound on for the match sound. 6 to 8 seconds. Cut on "Board complete!". Works as a vertical reel if cropped to the board.

Caption: Flip. Match. MATCH!  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: the chase  (`footage-02`)

Right after a board, the monster appears in camp. Third person: run at it, miss as it hops away to a new spot, turn, sprint, catch it on the next hop for the sparkle burst. Keep the "Chances" counter visible. 10 to 12 seconds. Cut on the catch burst. Vertical reel: keep the monster in the middle third.

Caption: 5 chances. It knows.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: Monster Collected!  (`footage-03`)

Catch a Common or Rare monster, hold on the "Monster Collected!" toast, then open the Monster Codex and show the new monster in its row. 6 to 8 seconds. Cut before the Epic row comes into view; never show an unlocked Epic.

Caption: One more for the Codex.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: the last 10 seconds  (`footage-04`)

Play a later board (use Select checkpoint) and let the timer drop under 10 seconds so it starts blinking red. Make the last match with 1 to 2 seconds left, or let it run out to "Time's up". Full-screen capture, sound on for the countdown. 6 to 10 seconds. Cut on the result.

Caption: Watch the timer. Not the cards. Wait. Watch the cards.  [FOOTAGE]

### Footage: 30-second trailer  (`footage-05`)

Trailer, 25 to 30 seconds, 16:9 1920x1080, plus a 9:16 vertical cut. 0-3 s: a monster hopping in camp with the player running at it (motion in the first three seconds). 3-10 s: fast Memory Match flips with "MATCH!" popups. 10-18 s: the chase, a miss, then the catch burst. 18-23 s: "Monster Collected!" and the Codex filling. 23-27 s: the weekly leaderboard. 27-30 s: the key art with the link. Captions on screen so it works muted. Keep the Epic monsters out.

Caption: Monster Recon: Halloween Edition. Flip. Match. Run.  [FOOTAGE]  https://decentraland.org/jump/?realm=monsterrecon.dcl.eth
