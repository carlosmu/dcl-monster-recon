### Short description (136 characters)

Flip cards against the clock in Memory Match, then chase the monster you won around camp. Collect all 20 and top the weekly leaderboard.

### Long description

Every checkpoint is a timed Memory Match board. Clear it and a monster appears in camp, hopping from spot to spot: you get five chances to catch it before it gets away. Twenty monsters fill your Monster Codex, from Common to Epic.

Your best times go on a weekly leaderboard that resets every Monday at 10:00 UTC, with weekly rewards for players who complete the mission. Right now the camp is running its Halloween Edition.

## Behind the scenes

### Behind the scenes: the difficulty curve  (`bts-01`)

> How Monster Recon gets harder:
>
> The monster's hop timer starts at 12 seconds and drops 1 second every two levels, down to 3 seconds from level 19 on.
>
> The boards tighten too: 20 seconds on the very first one, 13 on checkpoint 2's first board.
>
> #gamedev #SDK7

### Behind the scenes: the server keeps score  (`bts-02`)

> Leaderboard times and weekly reward claims in Monster Recon don't trust the player's client. They go through an authoritative server that checks the mission before a claim is registered.

> The week rolls over Monday 10:00 UTC, and past weeks stay archived.
